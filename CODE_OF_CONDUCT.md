# Community conduct

Keep project spaces useful, respectful and safe for people with different
backgrounds and experience levels. Critique ideas and code constructively, assume
good intent, and respect stated boundaries.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexual harassment, impersonation, doxxing,
spam and publishing another person's private information are not acceptable.
Do not post credentials, customer data or exploit-sensitive reports publicly.

The maintainer may edit/remove content, close discussions, or limit participation
in response to violations. Consequences should be proportionate to the behavior
and its impact. No participant is exempt from these expectations.

For abuse requiring privacy or account/platform intervention, use GitHub's
"Report abuse" controls. Do not post private complaints or personal information
in a public issue. Security vulnerabilities belong in the repository's private
vulnerability reporting channel described in SECURITY.md. A project-specific
private conduct-reporting channel has not yet been established.
